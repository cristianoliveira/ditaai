Voices

Adds variety: every new page picks a different installed voice, so long reading sessions do not sound monotonous. Off keeps your selected voice.

Synthesis

How carefully DitaAi narrates each sentence. Quality is the number of denoising steps used to synthesize the voice: more steps sound cleaner and more natural but take longer to generate. Balanced (8 steps) is a good default; use Fast (4) for long pages and Best quality (16) when you want the most polished audio. Takes effect on the next utterance.

How much narration DitaAi prepares before playback. A larger buffer reduces pauses after short paragraphs but takes longer to start. Immediate start keeps current one-paragraph lookahead behavior.

The language DitaAi narrates in. Voices are multilingual, so pick the language of the page you are reading (English by default). Takes effect on the next utterance.

Keyboard shortcuts

Control narration from anywhere on the page. Click Change, then press the new keys.

Configuration

Back up or restore shortcuts, pronunciations, reading preferences, voice selection, and page scopes. Downloaded voice models are not included.
