Microphone Permissions Guide
If you see a pop-up
Click “Allow while visiting the site” to start using Speechify instantly. Speak instead of typing.
If you don’t see a pop-up
Click the microphone icon and select “Always allow” and then click “Done”.
If you don’t see the microphone icon
Refresh the page, then click the microphone icon and select “Always allow” and then click “Done”.

Access Granted

You can close this tab and use microphone for Voice Mode or Dictation

Microphone Access Needed

Click the icon and select “Always allow” and then click “Done”.
If you don’t see the icon, click to refresh the page, then try again.
Speechify only accesses your microphone when you click the talk button—never in the background.

Microphone Access Needed

Please allow Speechify to access your microphone so you can start talking.
